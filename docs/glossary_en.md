# 📖 Glossary

Here you will find the most important terms related to WhisperX Adaptive Learning – explained simply.

---

- **Adaptive Learning**
  > A process where the system adapts to individual speakers and their pronunciation.

- **Speaker Profile**
  > A collection of voice samples and metadata assigned to a person.

- **Transcription**
  > Converting spoken language into written text.

- **Model Adaptation**
  > Fine-tuning the speech recognition model to individual speakers.

- **Voice Profile**
  > Another term for speaker profile.

- **Batch Transcription**
  > Automatic processing of many audio files in one step.

- **Feedback Loop**
  > Feeding corrections back into the system to further improve profiles and models.

- **CLI (Command Line Interface)**
  > Command line tool for controlling WhisperX.

- **API**
  > Programming interface for integrating WhisperX into your own applications.

- **Enhance Audio**
  > Option to improve audio quality before transcription.

- **Profile Folder**
  > Storage location for all speaker profiles (`~/.cache/whisperx/voice_profiles`).
